What you’ll find here

Modern Fantasy Shaped by Three Foundations

  • Identity

    Who we become matters more than the things we possess. The things to which we anchor our identity ultimately shape our course.

  • Stewardship

    The world does not revolve around any of us. We are entrusted with people, places, stories, and responsibilities worthy of our care and respect.

  • Hope

    Our world is a broken place, and it will never be exactly as it once was. Yet even in the face of darkness and loss, there remains a path toward restoring what truly matters, piece by piece.

Why I Write

Author Statement

I believe stories matter because ideas have consequences. The stories we tell, the values we embrace, and the choices we make all shape who we become. My fiction explores those consequences through worlds of wonder, sacrifice, responsibility, and hope.
