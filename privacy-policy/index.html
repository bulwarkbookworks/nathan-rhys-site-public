Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to me. This website exists to share information about my books, artwork, writing, and related projects. I limit the personal information I request, and I do not sell personal information.

This website is owned and operated by Christopher Goehrs, a Wisconsin sole proprietor doing business as Bulwark Bookworks and writing under the pen name Nathan Rhys. In this policy, “I,” “me,” and “my” refer to the site operator.

Cloudflare Web Analytics

Analytics

This website uses Cloudflare Web Analytics to help me understand how visitors use the site and how well it performs.

Information collected may include:

  • Pages visited

  • Approximate geographic region

  • Browser and operating system

  • Referring website

  • General usage and performance information

Cloudflare Web Analytics does not use cookies or local storage to collect usage metrics and is designed not to fingerprint individual visitors. The information available to me is aggregated and is not intended to identify individual visitors.

Website Hosting and Content Delivery

This website is hosted through GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes.

Certain images and other site assets may be delivered through Sanity. When a visitor’s browser requests content from Sanity, Sanity may process technical information such as the originating IP address, browser information, access time, and requested content for delivery, security, and troubleshooting.

Contact Form

If you contact me through this website, I may collect the information you choose to provide, including:

  • Your name

  • Your email address

  • The subject of your inquiry

  • The contents of your message

The contact form is processed through Web3Forms, which receives the submission and delivers it to my email provider.

The form also uses Cloudflare Turnstile to distinguish legitimate visitors from automated submissions. Turnstile may process technical information about your browser, device, network connection, and interaction with the form for security purposes.

Please do not submit sensitive personal, financial, medical, or confidential information through the contact form.

Newsletter

If you choose to subscribe to my newsletter, you will be asked to provide your email address.

The newsletter is managed through MailerLite. MailerLite may process your email address, signup information, IP address, browser or device information, and information about email delivery and engagement, such as whether an email was opened or a link was selected.

I use this information to:

  • Send the updates you requested

  • Manage newsletter subscriptions

  • Maintain the security and deliverability of the mailing list

  • Understand general newsletter performance

Your email address will only be used for updates related to my books, writing, and other projects you have chosen to receive.

You may unsubscribe at any time using the link included in every newsletter email.

Cookies and Similar Technologies

Cloudflare Web Analytics does not use cookies or local storage to measure website traffic.

Other necessary services used by this website, including security tools and embedded forms, may use cookies or similar browser technologies when required to provide their functions. I do not use advertising cookies or sell information for targeted advertising.

Newsletter emails may contain tracking pixels or specially formatted links that allow MailerLite to report delivery and engagement information.

I do not sell, rent, or trade personal information.

Information may be shared with service providers only as necessary to operate this website, process contact messages, provide security, and deliver the newsletter. These providers include:

  • Cloudflare
  • GitHub
  • Sanity
  • Web3Forms
  • MailerLite
  • My email provider

I may also disclose information when reasonably necessary to comply with the law, respond to valid legal process, prevent fraud or abuse, protect the security of the website, or protect the rights and safety of others.

Data Retention

I retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected.

  • Newsletter information is ordinarily retained while you remain subscribed. Limited information may be retained afterward when necessary to honor an unsubscribe request or comply with legal obligations.
  • Contact messages may be retained as long as reasonably necessary to respond, maintain appropriate business records, resolve disputes, or comply with legal obligations.
  • Service providers may retain information according to their respective policies and service arrangements.

You may request deletion of personal information I control, subject to any legal or recordkeeping obligations that require its retention.

Your Choices and Requests

You may:

  • Unsubscribe from the newsletter using the link in any newsletter email.
  • Ask whether I maintain personal information about you.
  • Request correction or deletion of personal information I control.
  • Ask questions about how your information is used.

Depending on where you live, applicable law may provide additional privacy rights.

Requests may be submitted through the Contact page. I may need to verify your identity before completing certain requests.

Children’s Privacy

This website is not directed to children under 13, and I do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

If you believe a child under 13 has submitted personal information through this website, please contact me so that I can take appropriate steps to remove it.

Data Security

I use reasonable administrative and technical measures to protect the information submitted through this website. However, no internet transmission, email system, or storage service can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

This website may contain links to websites operated by other parties. This Privacy Policy does not govern those websites, and I am not responsible for their privacy practices.
