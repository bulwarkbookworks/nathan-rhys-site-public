Meet the Characters

  • Emerald Monteverde

    Reluctant Newcomer

    She didn’t plan on finding a hidden garden. She didn’t plan on meeting a mysterious caretaker or ancient guardians. And she certainly didn’t plan on fighting corrupt creatures whose essence destroys everything it touches.

    Emerald didn’t plan on any of that. But she’s willing to do whatever it takes to protect what she loves.

  • The Caretaker

    Keeper of the Garden's secrets

    The Caretaker watches over a garden most of the world doesn't know exists.

    He is warm, patient, and frustratingly selective about the questions he's willing to answer.

    When Emerald finds her way inside the Garden, he doesn't tell her what to believe or what choice to make. Instead, he offers her something much more difficult:

    A choice that is genuinely hers.

  • Tzuriel

    Guardian of the Garden

    Tzuriel has protected the Garden from dangers most people won’t—and shouldn’t—know about.

    Towering and decidedly inhuman, he is one of the Guardians. But beneath an intimidating exterior, Emerald discovers someone remarkably patient and gentle.

    He may be one of the strangest people Emerald has ever met.

    And one of the easiest to trust.
